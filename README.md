# 🌍 Kuson Globe Compare

**True Spherical 3D Country Size Superimposition & Vital Intelligence Platform**

**Kuson Globe Compare** lets you superimpose any country (defaulting to **Thailand 🇹🇭**) onto a 3D WebGL globe, drag and rotate it across continents with **zero projection distortion**, and inspect head-to-head vital statistics, counterfactual simulations, expat/nomad metrics, and shareable infographics against the country directly underneath.

## ✨ Highlights

- **True 3D Spherical Geometry (`d3-geo` + `Three.js`)**: Preserves 100% of true spherical surface area and geodesic proportions anywhere on Earth, eliminating Web Mercator latitude distortion.
- **6 Instant 3D Globe Layers**:
  1. **Geographic (`Geographic Blue Marble`)** — Bathymetric oceans, continental shelves, biomes, deserts, and alpine ridges.
  2. **3D Terrain (`3D Topographic Relief`)** — Hypsometric elevation tinting with 3D mountain bump relief and contours.
  3. **Political Map (`Editorial Political Atlas`)** — Distinct sovereign nation cartography with Equator/Tropic lines and graticules.
  4. **Night Lights (`Orbital Night Lights`)** — Nocturnal urban density illumination across 120+ global metropolitan corridors.
  5. **Data Heatmap (`Global Statistical Choropleth`)** — Live heatmap switchable between GDP per Capita, Population Density, Nominal GDP, Human Development Index (HDI), and Land Area.
  6. **Hologram (`Tactical Geodesic Grid`)** — High-contrast cybernetic command globe.
- **4 Persona Intelligence Hubs**:
  - **Vital Stats**: 5-Dimension Scale Fingerprint radar chart, proportional bars (Area, Population, Density, Nominal & PPP GDP, GDP/Capita, HDI, Life Expectancy, Elevation, Coastline), and multi-country footprint detection.
  - **What-If Lab**: Live **Mercator Projection Mythbuster** ($\sec^2(\phi)$ area inflation visualizer) plus counterfactual population density and GDP per capita swap simulations.
  - **Nomad & Flight Hub**: Great-Circle geodesic displacement & flight duration calculator, interactive **Purchasing Power Parity (PPP)** monthly budget slider, currency, driving side, timezone, and top export pillars.
  - **Scale Quiz & Curated Tours**: 1-click guided comparisons (*Thailand over Western Europe*, *Thailand over UK*, *Thailand over US East Coast*, *Thailand over Japan*, *Greenland over Africa*, *Indonesia over USA*, *Chile across Europe*) and an interactive educator quiz.
- **Viral Infographic & Social Share Studio**: 1-click export of a `1600×920` `.PNG` comparison poster combining the live 3D globe canvas with a branded scorecard, plus copyable interactive 3D deep links.
